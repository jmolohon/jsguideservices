Mille Lacs Lake is a 132,516-acre glacially-formed lake in east-central Minnesota. Even though most visitors come for the world class fishing, many say the amazing view of such a massive pristine lake is worth the trip alone. The Mille Lacs Lake game fish population is primarily composed of walleye, yellow perch, northern pike, muskellunge, and smallmouth bass. Approximately 70 percent of the 85 miles of shoreline consists of suitable walleye spawning habitat. This makes the lake a virtual factory. Look no further for a trip of a lifetime.
