Welcome to Joe Scegura’s Guide Service

Do you like to fish? Would you like to learn how to catch more fish? Or maybe your free time is limited and you just don’t have time to find the fish on your own?

From experienced fishing enthusiast to novice angler, you will have nothing short of a great time fishing with us.

Joe Scegura’s Guide Service offers guided fishing trips across Central Minnesota, primarily on Alexandria area lakes, Mille Lacs Lake, and the northern part of the Mississippi River. We fish all species, but specialize in walleye. Whether you are looking to hone a special technique, learn the basics, or just catch some fish…give us a call!
