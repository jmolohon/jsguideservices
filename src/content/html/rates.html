We offer full day and half day trips. The rates are based on a minimum of two anglers per boat. Our boats can comfortably and safely fish up to four clients. If you have additional people that would like to ride along to experience the fun there would be an additional fee of $50 per person (up to 6 total people). We work with a number of other top guides across the state, so if you have a larger group of 5-15 people we can certainly accommodate you.

This is your day so let us know if you have any special requests and we’ll do everything we can to accommodate you!
