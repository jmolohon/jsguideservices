What we Provide:

All top of the line equipment. From the boat to the bait, we cut no corners. This is your day on the water and we want it to be the best it can possibly be!

All guide boats are 18′ – 20′ tiller fishing boats. Joe’s boat is a 20′ Ranger tiller with a 115 Yamaha motor. This boat fishes 3 clients very comfortably, but will allow fishing for up to 4-5 clients. The boat is equipped with Minn Kota trolling motors and Lowrance electronics. All rods and reels are also premium quality.

All gas, bait and tackle for the day will be provided.

If you desire to keep fish, we will gladly clean and bag them for you.

What you should bring:

Current Minnesota Fishing License. (Buy MN fishing license here)
Proper clothing for the weather: Jacket, hat, raingear, etc.
Note: It can feel much colder on the water.
Camera
Sunscreen
Drinks and Snacks
Cooler with ice for fish, if you desire to keep any.
