Hello my name is Joe Scegura and welcome to my site. I was born and raised in the Central Minnesota area. I have been addicted to fishing for as long as I can remember. When all the other kids were playing ball, I was driving my bike two miles to the lake near my home to fish. I bought my first boat when I was 13 years old, and I’ve fished just about every day since. I’ve long since sold that boat and now run a twenty foot Ranger with a 150 Yamaha. This tiller boat, along with my extensive knowledge of Central Minnesota, ensures you a comfortable and productive day on the water that you won’t soon forget. My favorite methods of fishing include: live bait rigging, bobber fishing, trolling spinners, and pulling crank baits. I love “talking fish”, so I’m more than willing to answer any questions you might have about any type of fishing. Give me a call or shoot me an email anytime, I’m always ready to fish!
